Clay is a composite KDE theme and contains independently licensed components.

- Files authored specifically for Clay use the license declared in their local `metadata.json`.
- `payload/kwin/tabbox/clay_grid/contents/ui/main.qml` is derived from KDE KWin's Thumbnail Grid and retains its SPDX GPL-2.0-or-later header.
- Files under `payload/plasma/look-and-feel/*/contents/logout/` are derived from KDE Breeze Look-and-Feel and retain their original SPDX headers.
- Clay wallpapers declare CC0-1.0 in their metadata.

The original SPDX headers inside derived source files are authoritative for those files.
